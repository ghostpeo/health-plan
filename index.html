Week 1 — 3 Sets × 5 Reps

Foundation week. Focus on movement quality above all. Sets at 65% / 75% / 85% of working max. Leave at least one rep in the tank on every set — especially Tuesday before Wednesday tennis.

Session C

Saturday — Home Gym

~55 min · Best buffer day — no sport before or after

🏠 Home

WARM-UP — 10 MIN


Light Cardio + Hip Prep

3 min cardio · Cat-cow · Glute bridges × 10

MAIN LIFT — KB DEADLIFT


Double KB Deadlift

Week 1: 3×5 at 65% / 75% / 85% · Rest 2–3 min

Two 20kg KBs = 40kg total. If too light, switch to single-leg variant.


Watch

SUPPLEMENTAL


DB Floor Press

3×8 · Flat on floor, elbows at 45° · Full pause at bottom

Floor limits range slightly — combine with ring push-ups for full depth.


Watch


Ring Rows

3×10 · Adjust angle to control difficulty · Full arm extension

More horizontal = harder. Great horizontal pull to balance all pressing.


Watch

PUSH + PULL — VOLUME BLOCK


Deep Push-Ups on DBs

4×12–15 · DBs as handles · Pause 1 sec at bottom stretch

Handles let you go deeper than floor. This is your chest volume.


Watch


Pull-Ups

4 sets × max reps · Rest 2 min · Push for more than Tuesday

Saturday is your best recovery buffer — this is where you push harder.


Watch


KB Swings

3×15 · 20kg · Hip hinge explosive — not a squat

Posterior chain power. Great carryover to racket sports and golf.


Watch

MOBILITY — DON'T SKIP


Pigeon Pose or Figure-4 Stretch

2 min each side · Hip external rotation


Watch


World's Greatest Stretch

5 reps each side · Full body mobility in one movement


Watch

🎯 FLEXIBILITY GOAL — TOUCH THE FLOOR


Standing Forward Fold — Hold & Breathe

3 × 60 sec · Knees soft at first, straighten gradually · Let gravity do the work

Stage 1: Fingertips to floor. Stage 2: Palms flat. Stage 3: Forehead to thighs. Never force — relax into it.


Watch


Seated Single-Leg Forward Fold

2 × 60 sec each leg · Reach toward foot · Flex foot back

Targets each hamstring independently. Often one side is tighter — note which.


Watch


PNF Hamstring Stretch — Contract & Release

3 × per leg · Contract 6 sec, release, reach further, hold 30 sec

Fastest flexibility gains available. Contract = push heel into floor or band. Release = go deeper.


Watch


Wall Hamstring Stretch — Legs Up

1 × 3 min · Lie on back, legs straight up wall · Shuffle hips close to wall

Passive and relaxing — perfect end to the session. Let gravity decompress the spine too.
